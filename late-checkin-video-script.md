# Late Check-In Demo Video Script (about 90 seconds)

Audience: front desk / reservations staff at small Cloudbeds properties, who will
forward it to the owner. One story only: the guest who shows up after the desk
closes. No reservation-agent features.

Recording setup: phone screen recording for scenes 3-5 (real flow, real texts),
voiceover recorded over it. Scenes 1, 2 and 6 can be simple stills or text on a
plain background.

| # | Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00-0:10 | Text: "11:40 PM." A dark entrance, locked door. | It's 11:40 at night. Your desk closed at 10. A guest just got off a late flight and is standing at your door. |
| 2 | 0:10-0:22 | Text: "Booked on Expedia. Never saw the check-in email." | They booked through Expedia. Your check-in email went to a relay address, and they never saw it. No code, nobody on site. Normally that's a cancelled booking, a refund, or someone on your team getting woken up. |
| 3 | 0:22-0:35 | Sign at the entrance: "Arriving late? Scan here or call." Phone scans the QR code. | Instead, there's a sign at the entrance. The guest scans the code, or calls the number. |
| 4 | 0:35-0:60 | Real screen recording of the text thread: the AI asks for their name, finds the reservation, and sends the check-in steps and documents. | An assistant answers right away. It finds their reservation by name and texts them the same check-in steps and documents your team would normally send. |
| 5 | 0:60-0:75 | The guest finishes the steps, and the self check-in instructions arrive by text. | When they finish, the self check-in instructions go out automatically. They're inside in a few minutes. |
| 6 | 0:75-0:90 | Text: "Works with Cloudbeds. Nobody has to be awake for it." Then Chris's name and Virtual Office AI. | It works with Cloudbeds, you keep the booking, and nobody on your team has to be awake for it. If late arrivals ever cost you a booking or a night's sleep, reply to my email and I'll show you how it's set up. |

## Rules for the recording

- Show the real product only. Every text on screen should be a real message
  the system sends. Don't stage features it doesn't have.
- Use a test reservation and a test name. No real guest data on screen.
- Say "works with Cloudbeds". Never "partner" or "official".
- Keep it under 90 seconds. If it runs long, cut scene 2 down first.
- End on "reply to my email", not a link or phone number, so it fits the
  one-to-one reply threads.

## Shot list (for scenes 3-5)

1. Print a sign: "Arriving late? Scan here or call (number)". Tape it to a door.
2. Record your phone screen. Scan the QR code from the sign.
3. Go through the flow as a guest with a test reservation in Cloudbeds.
4. Keep the recording running until the self check-in instructions arrive.
5. Record the voiceover separately and lay it over the top (Canva, CapCut or
   iMovie all do this).
